Privacy Policy

Water Sort Puzzle: Sip Sort · Effective 21 October 2026

Sip Sort is a puzzle game made by Sush Builds ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: you don't create an account, we don't run servers that collect your data, and your progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses some device information to show and measure ads.

What stays on your phone

Your levels, coins, unlocked glasses, cafés and drink menus, and settings (sound, vibration) are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the game deletes them.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details. The game has no sign-in and no chat.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, it shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android), where you allow it;
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location (city or region);
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps) and diagnostic data such as crashes inside the ad software.

Google handles this information under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Rewarded videos are always your choice: you tap to watch one in return for coins or tools.

Your choices

  • EU, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time from Privacy → Ad choices on the game's home screen.
  • iPhone: the game asks whether it may use your advertising identifier (Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", it isn't used. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Choosing not to share means you still see ads; they're just less tailored to you.

United States residents

We don't sell your personal information for money. Showing personalised ads may count as "sharing" under some state laws (such as California's). You can opt out using the device settings above.

Children

Sip Sort is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been collected, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Changes

If we change how the game uses information (for example by adding purchases or analytics), we'll update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the game.
